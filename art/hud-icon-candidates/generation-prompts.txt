studded-tools

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate an original production game HUD sprite atlas for Mine EVERYTHING!, a Roblox mining simulator in Citrine Cove. It is one coordinated FIVE-icon family. Landscape canvas 3:2, exactly 3 columns by 2 rows in an implied regular grid of SQUARE cells, no actual grid. Place one icon at the exact center of each of the first five equal cells; cell six bottom-right is COMPLETELY EMPTY and transparent. All five badge outer silhouettes have the same size (roughly 80% of the cell) and substantial clear gaps, equally large glyphs and matched visual weight. Genuine transparent RGBA everywhere outside the five individual badges. Icons will be extracted and shown at 38px and 58px, so build broad solid faces, purposeful silhouettes, few large color masses. No text, no labels, no letters or numbers or interface mockups, no watermarks, no notification dots, no cast shadows on surrounding canvas, no extra objects between cells. Row-major sequence strictly Inventory, Skills, Achievements, Index, Settings. Each category has its own clear color identity. Direction: STUDDED TOOLS. Take cues from the Roblox capture of a bright two-column studded button grid featuring Index, Clans and Pass. Bold, hand-inked toy illustration with a slightly tilted chunky object, stepped cel-shaded faces, consistent midnight-navy exterior contours. One distinct bright colored rounded-square backing tile per icon with a restrained hint of four molded square studs near corners, heavier dark lower edge. Keep the central object at least 72% of the tile width, and strongly contrast with the plate. Top left: a marigold prospecting backpack with a broad flap, carry loop, chunky buckle and teal side roll on an orange-coral tile. Top middle: mint green crystal nodes in an unmistakable branching Y skill tree, chunky connected stems holding three clearly separate gems on a deep emerald tile. Top right: a broad gold trophy cup with two open handles and a single central diamond-shaped inset, on a dark grape-violet tile. Bottom left: a chunky closed cobalt field journal at a slight tilt, pale page block and a prominent single aqua ore crystal emblem on its blue front cover, on a light turquoise tile. Bottom middle: a large pale steel-blue 8-tooth settings cog with a clean very dark hub on a slate-blue tile. Make it feel like a charming finished simulator HUD, crisp high-contrast mobile illustration. No tiny decorative greebles or random sparkles.
